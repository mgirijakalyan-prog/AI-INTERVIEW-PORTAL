About AI Interview Prep

An AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students improve their technical knowledge, communication skills, and interview confidence.

What is AI Interview Prep?

AI Interview Prep is a web-based interview preparation portal developed to help students prepare for technical and HR interviews. The platform provides practice questions, mock interviews, performance tracking, and personalized feedback.

Key Features

📝 Practice Questions

Practice questions from Frontend, JavaScript, Programming, Database, HR, and Aptitude categories.

🎤 Mock Interview

Simulate an interview environment and receive feedback based on your answers.

📊 Performance Tracking

Track practice sessions, scores, interview performance, and progress through the dashboard.

🔐 Student Login

Students can log in to access their personalized interview preparation dashboard.

💡 Preparation Tips

Get useful interview preparation tips to improve confidence and communication.

🤖 AI Evaluation

The system can evaluate interview answers and provide feedback using AI technology.

Technologies Used

The project is developed using modern web technologies.

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript LocalStorage AI API

Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to provide students with an accessible platform where they can practice interview questions, simulate interviews, identify their weak areas, and improve their overall interview performance.
